How Undo402 works

01

Pay into escrow

Buyer approves USDC and locks it in Undo402Escrow on Base Sepolia. The Base tx hash becomes payment_proof on GenLayer.

02

Deliver & accept

Seller submits a delivery URL. Buyer accepts (fast path, no jury) — or both propose the same outcome (2-of-2 mutual agreement).

03

Dispute → AI jury

Validators fetch the delivery independently and judge it against the agreed criteria. Exact decision match required. undetermined = retryable, never silently swallowed.

04

Settle on Base

Relay reads verdict + SHA-256 attestation and calls release / refund / releasePartial on the Base escrow. USDC moves to the winner.

Anti-stuck design

  • Buyer timeout claim on Base escrow — funds can never be locked forever
  • Evidence deadline + default judgment — silence is not a strategy
  • Prompt-injection defense (STEP 0) — delivery content is data, not instructions
  • Human override layer — owner can settle disputed cases without AI
